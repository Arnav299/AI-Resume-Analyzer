# AI Resume Analyzer - User Guide

Welcome to the AI Resume Analyzer & Career Recommendation Portal! This guide will help you understand how to use the portal to evaluate your resume, discover career recommendations, and understand your ATS score.

## 1. Getting Started

1. **Access the Portal**: Open the application URL in your web browser.
2. **Login/Register**: If authentication is enabled, please register for an account or log in. This allows you to track your past resume analyses in your dashboard.

## 2. Uploading Your Resume

1. Navigate to the **Upload** section from the top navigation bar.
2. Click on the file dropzone or drag and drop your resume file.
   - **Supported Format:** PDF (`.pdf`) only.
   - **Note on Scanned PDFs:** Text-based PDFs yield the best results. If you upload a scanned image PDF, the system will attempt to use OCR to read it, but accuracy may vary.
3. Click the **Analyze Resume** button.
4. Wait a few moments while the AI extracts your details, analyzes your skills, and generates recommendations.

## 3. Understanding the Analysis Results

Once the analysis is complete, you will be redirected to the results page, which is broken down into several tabs/sections:

### ATS Score
This is a score out of 100 indicating how well your resume is formatted and optimized for Applicant Tracking Systems. 
- A high score (80+) means your resume is easily readable by machines and contains good keyword density.

### Extracted Information
Review the information the system successfully extracted:
- **Contact Info:** Name, Email, Phone.
- **Skills:** Both Hard Skills and Soft Skills.
- **Experience & Education:** A parsed list of your background.
*Tip: If anything is missing, consider reformatting that section in your resume to be clearer.*

### Skill Gap Analysis
The AI compares your extracted skills against your identified potential roles and highlights:
- **Missing Skills:** Skills you should consider learning or highlighting more clearly.
- **Recommendations:** Actionable advice on how to bridge the gap.

### Career Recommendations
Based on your current skill set and experience, the AI suggests suitable career paths or roles you are a strong match for.

## 4. Dashboard

Navigate to the **Dashboard** to view:
- A history of all your uploaded resumes.
- Trending metrics of your ATS scores over time.
- A quick overview of your top strengths and persistent skill gaps.

## 5. Providing Feedback

If you notice that an extraction was inaccurate or a career recommendation wasn't relevant:
1. Scroll to the **Feedback** section at the bottom of your analysis report.
2. Submit your corrections or comments.
3. Your feedback helps improve the AI models for future analysis!
